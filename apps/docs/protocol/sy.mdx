## What SY represents

SY shares are minted by `sy_wrapper` against a deposited underlying asset. Every deposit is immediately supplied 100% into a Blend Capital lending pool (`deposit()`, calling `pool_client.submit(...)` with `BLEND_REQUEST_TYPE_SUPPLY`). SY is the accounting unit for "a claim on the underlying plus whatever yield the Blend pool has generated."

<Warning>
  **SY units are not the same as underlying units.** SY shares are minted at the current exchange rate, and that rate only increases over time. 1 SY share is worth ≥ 1 unit of underlying, never less, by construction (barring an admin-invoked `mark_loss`).
</Warning>

## Exchange rate

```text
exchange_rate = TotalUnderlying * EXCHANGE_RATE_SCALAR / TotalShares
EXCHANGE_RATE_SCALAR = 1_000_000_000   // 1e9, not WAD/1e18
```

- `TotalUnderlying` is reconciled against `total_backing()` (idle contract balance + `pool_supplied_value()`) inside `refresh_rate()`.
- The rate **ratchets up only** — it is never allowed to decrease on a `refresh_rate()` call, and increases are capped at **10% per call** regardless of what the pool reports. This is a deliberate defense (referred to in code comments as the "Fix H5" clamp) against a compromised or dishonest pool suddenly reporting an inflated balance.
- `mark_loss()` is the only way the rate can go down, and it is strictly admin-gated — an explicit, auditable event, not something that happens automatically from pool data.

## AUM (`pool_supplied_value`)

```text
total_backing() = idle_contract_balance + pool_supplied_value()
pool_supplied_value() = sum of Blend positions' reported `supply` (1:1 as underlying)
```

<Note>
  This is a **documented approximation**, not exact Blend `b_rate` accounting. The code's own comment explains that Blend bTokens accrue value via a `b_rate` obtained from the pool's `get_reserve` call, but `sy_wrapper` could not confirm `get_reserve`'s exact `ReserveData` field layout with confidence, and deliberately chose to treat reported `supply` as 1:1 underlying rather than guess a field layout it wasn't sure of. The stated effect is under-reporting yield (safe direction), not over-reporting it.
</Note>

## Deposit and redemption

<Tabs>
  <Tab title="Deposit">
    `deposit(from, amount) -> i128` — pulls `amount` of underlying from `from`, supplies it into the Blend pool via `submit()`, mints SY shares at the current exchange rate, and returns the shares minted.
  </Tab>
  <Tab title="Withdraw">
    `withdraw(from, shares) -> i128` — burns `shares`, withdraws the owed underlying from the Blend pool via `submit()` with a `WITHDRAW` request type, and returns the underlying amount released.
  </Tab>
</Tabs>

Other entry points: `refresh_rate()` (permissionless — reconciles and ratchets the rate), `harvest_yield()` (admin), `mark_loss()` (admin, can only decrease `TotalUnderlying`), `pause()`/`unpause()` (admin), `transfer_admin`/`accept_admin` (two-step), `preview_deposit`, `preview_withdraw`, `get_exchange_rate()`, `total_shares()`, `underlying_asset()`.

## Why the SY/underlying distinction matters for the AMM

`marketplace` reserves and pricing operate on the underlying's own units after `tokenizer` converts SY shares into PT/YT at 1:1 against SY at the time of minting. Any code reading `sy_wrapper.get_exchange_rate()` and treating it as a WAD (1e18) value rather than a 1e9 value will be off by 9 orders of magnitude — always check `EXCHANGE_RATE_SCALAR` at the call site.
